## There are Better Linux Distros so Don't Use This

If you use Omarchy for practical reasons, you will probably be better served by alternatives.

If you want a preconfigured Arch based Linux distro, go with [CachyOS](https://cachyos.org) or [EndeavourOS](https://endeavouros.com). That is if you need Arch and don't want to go through the work of configuring it from scratch. There are many other Linux distros that likely serve normal computer users better. [Fedora](https://fedoraproject.org) and [Linux Mint](https://www.linuxmint.com) are what I normally recommend for most people. Mint in particular is great for non-techies and Fedora gives a stable, but up to date distro for people who are willing to put a little work into their system (to get non-free drivers).

There are many Hyperland configurations online that aren't preloaded with pro-AI and neo-nazi biases. So even if you want to use Hyperland you can without DHH's bloatware.
